Bio Solar Panel

Team: Team Terra
Reference ID: SRCW-HHH2026-002

Problem Statement
Agricultural activities generate large quantities of biodegradable waste such as crop residues, fruit and vegetable waste, straw, and bagasse. Much of this waste is poorly managed or burned, causing pollution while valuable organic material remains underutilized. At the same time, farms increasingly require small amounts of clean electricity for sensors, monitoring systems, and other low-power applications.

Proposed Solution
LUMORA is an agricultural-waste-to-bioelectricity system that converts biodegradable farm waste into usable electrical energy through a bio-electrochemical process. Microorganisms break down the organic matter and release electrons, which are captured by electrodes and converted into electricity, then conditioned and stored for powering low-energy devices such as IoT sensors, monitoring systems, and LED lighting.
